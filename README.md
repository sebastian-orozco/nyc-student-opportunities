# NYC High School Student Opportunities 🗽

A searchable list of programs, internships, jobs, volunteering, competitions, scholarships, and events for New York City high school students, updated as new opportunities are announced.

Use **Ctrl/Cmd + F** to search by grade (e.g. `11`), borough, subject, or keyword. Every listing links straight to the program's own site.

🙏 **Know an opportunity that's missing, or spot something out of date? [Open an issue](../../issues/new/choose)!** See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

Looking for programs that already closed? Many run every year. See the **[Archive](ARCHIVE.md)**.

---

## Legend

| Symbol | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 🔐 | **Required**: you must meet this to apply |
| 🎯 | **Priority**: preferred, but all students may apply |
| 💰 | Pays students (wage or stipend) |
| 🎓 aid | Financial aid available |
| 🔁 | Rolling deadline: apply any time |
| ✈️ | Outside New York City |
| 🆕 | Added in the last 7 days |
| ⚠️ | Details conflict in the source; confirm on the program's site |

Within each section, the **latest deadlines are at the top** and the soonest are at the bottom. Rolling deadlines come last. Always confirm dates and eligibility on the program's website before applying.
